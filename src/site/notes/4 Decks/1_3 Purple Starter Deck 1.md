# Purple Starter Deck 1
iteration 10
![AV4_PurpleDeck1_BSSG009_1.png](/img/user/Images/AV4_PurpleDeck1_BSSG009_1.png)

So with the changes detailed in [[3 Design Theory/Balloon Design Strategy V2\|Balloon Design Strategy V2]], You'll notice that the bolt trio are all 1 cost balloons with 2 commitment pips. this means they cannot be played on the first turn but on your second turn onward you can start throwing them down with impunity. this has significantly helped the deck be more aggressive by allowing it to just send threats at players more readily, and without having to do heavy self milling to do so.

The -aga trio are also 3 cost balloons, again being more aggressively costed with more potent effects while having fragile to make them single use. Ice bolt and Blizzaga are also now this decks 2 blockers. Ice bolt being a 1 cost chump blocker while Blizzaga is a shredder blocker that can take down anything it blocks, but is fragile and can't wall out weaker stuff like other blockers.

The Spellbeast archetype is still an aggressive one, but is also leaning towards being more of a toolbox archetype. having some utility balloons that carry the ephemeral nature of this balloon class rather than everything strictly being an offensive tool to throw at enemy players.
