# Red Starter Deck
iteration 10
![AV4_RedDeck1_BSSG009_1.png](/img/user/Images/AV4_RedDeck1_BSSG009_1.png)

So this is with the changes in my design structure as shown in [[3 Design Theory/Balloon Design Strategy V2\|Balloon Design Strategy V2]].

Now Red tends to be the foundational color that anchors the others in design, so right off the bat it doesn't look like much is going on with the balloons. most of the changes are with Limits, With Yowie acting as a representative for a vanilla Red balloon, being a size 2 with a limit of 3.

Generally speaking, I'm designing around size 5 being a magic number for 2 and 3 cost balloons. where it's hard for them to feasibly reach past that size in limits. though red doesn't have the hardest time with it to be honest.

Another thing of note with the deck is that there's a lot of size 2 and 3 balloons but no size 4 balloons. They're not particularly desirable with this deck since it wants to play multiple threats or play something and have breath left over for dodging. the size 5's are expansions that can be reached easily enough.

Also there's **Hustle Band**. so effects where you draw a card upon doing something have typically been on balloons, and it used to be on **Broadnose**. however I found that a lot of the time you tend to not want to use the balloon for anything since you don't want to lose your draw engine. so they just kinda sit there. I figured at that point why not just make it an object?

the results were interesting to say the least. Hustle Band was way too easy to accumulate at a cost of 1 but at 2 it's manageable but a little inconvenient. you want it down early but playing it means not having a balloon to attack or block with. so you kinda have to decide if you really want it.

now, Broadnose losing the draw ability became a whole different balloon. now that he's only a 2 cost evasive it feels easier to just swing with him. and evasive lets him get around blockers and be tricky to deal with since he needs either a small blocker or a more specific answer. he honestly became a far more interesting card to play with because of that, and I've been thinking of leaving card draw engines onto objects for other decks.

Green has wind pendant. it costs 1 but kinda feels like a dud a lot of the time whenever I playtest it. but that's possibly because I play it against itself and a lot of it's wind spells aren't great against big balloons. Purple and Blue still rely on balloons for their card draw but I may switch to objects. purple probably doesn't need the card draw though.
