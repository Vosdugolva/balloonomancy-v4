# Blue Starter Deck 1
iteration 10
![AV4_BlueDeck1_BSSG009_1.png](/img/user/Images/AV4_BlueDeck1_BSSG009_1.png)

The Blue Deck is the newest deck relative to the other three, and while it has only gotten a few games under its belt it's proven itself to be a pretty stable deck for the most part, especially after I went for the Eastern / ninja direction that I initially went for with the blue deck back in version 3.

It's easy to tell that the deck is characterized more by its tools than it's balloons since those are the ones with placeholder art while none of the balloons have any yet. The design direction of blue's balloons are still being worked out, but currently it's action cards are what do most of the heavy lifting for the deck.

That's what makes blue as a color interesting, it's balloons aren't built to fight on the board because the player themselves do that with its battle actions and quick cards. So they mostly support the player with card draw, discard, and being blockers.

## Noteworthy cards:

### Smart boomerang
This idea was a slam dunk and one of my favorite cards in this game. A reusable battle action that's great for offense and triggering effects that respond to gadgets the also has a worthwhile caveat to think about. When played it goes to the top of your deck, so you'll draw it instead of whatever cards you would draw next. You can easily blackball yourself out of useful cards by using it too often.

The main use case with this is to lead in with attacking balloon to knock out a rival blocker, or when being rushed, you can use it to spend another rival balloon so that after that attack they can't attack with the next one.

### Stone guardian
A size 4 blocker that when played, immediately inflates itself for every stamina card you still have. Giving you upwards to a size 9 blocker the opponent has to deal with.
