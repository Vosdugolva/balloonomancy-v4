# LifeSaver
![BlueBalloon_5.png|300](/img/user/Images/BlueBalloon_5.png)

Originally was going to be a blue class division before the ninja pivot. now they're joining red as one of it's main balloon classes.

LifeSaver's are pooltoy inflatables that serve as top grade flotation devices for water play. They actually have existed before the sport of ballonomancy came into form, but now work double duty as participants in the dodgeball sport.

Lifesaver's are a red balloon class that leans more defensive, providing blockers, board protection, and discard recursion.
