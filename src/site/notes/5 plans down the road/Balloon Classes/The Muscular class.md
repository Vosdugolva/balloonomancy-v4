A red balloon class that best encapsulates the style's body centric nature. Big, top heavy and exceptionally muscular inflatables, they all possess a belt where a hose can be attached to inflate them with.

This class is currently more aesthetic driven than mechanics driven at the moment. Current mechanical considerations include them being interested in gaining and utilizing inflate tokens and having effects unlocked by being inflated to full capacity, or inheriting the action and stamina line focus from giant bats, while they double down on draw support and being fast balloons with tag team.
