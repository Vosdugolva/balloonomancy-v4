A purple balloon class and the inevitable Malevolified inspired archetype.

Large balloons that posses a number of mechanics to get them onto the field at a discount but with a different price. many will offer a reduced breath cost but either incur a penalty on play, or a perpetual one while your breath zone has less than their size.

they're an aggro archetype that tries to throw down big beaters at a lowered price and win before the penalties start to wear on the player.
