A red balloon class, Round and very toy like balloons noted for their elongated tails with a grip ring at the end of them.

The major mechanic of this balloon class is an ability called **pull back**, where anytime during your turn you may pay a breath cost to restore the balloon, and if done while the balloon is attacking then this cancels the attack. This means that these balloons can be restored repeatedly to attack over and over, and also have a built-in feint. Although this one is more telegraphed.
