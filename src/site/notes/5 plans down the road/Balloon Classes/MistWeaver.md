# MistWeaver
![BlueBalloon_4.png|290](/img/user/Images/BlueBalloon_4.png)

The other half of the pooltoy/water class that stays within blue and works within the more eastern theming of it. Mistweavers are large and ornate inflatables that take the shape of sea creatures, from fish to sea mammals to serpents to dragons even. These balloons posses magic that allows them to manipulate water to create dense fogs to swim through and conceal themselves under, or unleash blasts of water to disorient opponents.

Mistweaver's are like blue's analogue to Windsages, as they involve a lot of support for "Water Magic" actions like fog and vanish. along with evasion mechanics, Mistweavers deal in hard removal through shredder and spot removal that work relative to inflate tokens, targeting balloons that have under a certain amount.
