So balloon classes are typically committed to one color, being a product of that colors themes and style. Shared classes, or will I may occasionally call **intersectional classes** classes that just innately appear in one or more styles. Most of the time they are shared between two styles and contain mono colored balloons within both colors as well as some dual color balloons.

I like to call them intersectional because they represent gameplay and themes that combine the key elements of both colors into one cohesive archetype.
