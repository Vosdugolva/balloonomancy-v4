# Bloonsaur 

A planned intersectional class for red and green. Combining the physical play of red with the resource acceleration of green to perform a powerful beatdown archetype.

Bloonsaurs can be considered a hybrid of guardians and bigfoots. Favoring abilities like strength+x, blocker, intimidate, and abilities that let them attack other active balloons.


Elements from Red:
- strength+X
- forcing interaction on rival players

Elements from Green
- higher than average limits
